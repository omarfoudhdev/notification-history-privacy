Privacy Policy — Notification History

Last updated: 24 October 2026 (rev. 2)

In short: your notification history is stored only on your phone. We have no server, no account, and we never receive your messages.

1. Who we are

Notification History (Android package com.fddev.notificationhistory) is published by FDDev.

For any privacy question or request, write to omar.foudh.dev@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

2. What the app stores — on your device only

When you grant notification access, the app saves the following information, only for the apps you have selected (you can uncheck any app at any time):

  • the title and text of the notification, and the sender or conversation name when the app provides it;
  • the name and package of the app that posted it;
  • when it was received and, if applicable, when it was removed;
  • what you add yourself: favorites, notes, items moved to the vault or the trash;
  • images contained in notifications — only if you turn on image capture (off by default). They are stored encrypted on your device.

This data never leaves your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. There is no account and no sign-in.

3. Services provided by Google

The app itself sends none of your notification data. It includes the following Google components, which communicate with Google under Google's own policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) and the Google consent form (UMP): advertising identifier and device information used to serve ads. Ads are only loaded after the consent step; personalized ads only if you agree. How Google uses data from ads.
  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics: anonymous crash reports and anonymous usage statistics (technical diagnostics and general app usage, never notification content). They are always active to keep the app stable and improve it; they cannot be turned off in the app.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: a technical device token used to deliver service messages.
  • Google Play Billing: if you make a purchase, the payment is processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

None of these services receives the content of your notifications. Google Privacy Policy.

4. How long data is kept

You choose the retention period in Settings: 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, 1 year or unlimited (Pro). Older entries are deleted automatically. Items in the trash are permanently deleted after 30 days.

Uninstalling the app deletes all its data. Android backup is disabled for this app, so no copy is kept in the cloud or transferred to another phone.

5. Export

You can export your history (CSV, JSON, PDF or text) only when you explicitly ask for it. The file is saved where you choose and can be protected with a password (AES-256 encryption). Once exported, you are responsible for where you share that file.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access: your history is visible in the app.
  • Deletion: delete any entry, or everything at once from Settings.
  • Limitation: uncheck apps in “Apps”, or revoke notification access in Android settings.
  • Consent: change your advertising choices at any time in Settings → Privacy.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe), you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability. Since we hold no notification data, most of these are exercised directly in the app. For data processed by Google, see Google's policy or contact us at omar.foudh.dev@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

7. Security

The history is stored in the app's private storage, isolated by Android from other apps. Captured images are encrypted with a key kept in the Android Keystore. You can also enable a biometric lock for the app. Keep a screen lock on your phone: anyone with unlocked physical access to your device could read its content.

8. Other people's messages

Your notifications may contain messages written by other people. They stay on your device and we never see them. Use the app only on your own device, for your own notifications.

9. Children

The app is not intended for children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes

We will update this page if the app's behavior changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
